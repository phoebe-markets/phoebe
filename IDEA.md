phoebe.markets - bring leveraged trading to predictions markets

Exists as a layer on top of Polymarket.
Core functionality will be decentralized on chain (Polygon), with all the sugar existing in the web UI.
Traders can put up some USDC as collateral, and trade with higher volume borrowed from the Phoebe LP.
When a position's value dips below a calculated threshold, it gets liquidated.
