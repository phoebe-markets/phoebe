Risk engine

Leverage leaves before the jump.

Prediction markets don't drift, they jump, and every one ends at 0 or 1. So the allowed leverage is the tightest of three limits, each aimed at where the jumps happen.

Price band

Near 1 the only move left is a crash. Near 0 it's a lottery ticket.

Time to resolution

Ramps to zero a day out, so no leverage meets the final 0/1 jump.

Volatility

The margin cushion must cover a 2σ day plus a 10¢ jump allowance.

liquidation LTV = min( 85% × price band × time ramp , 1 − (2σ + 10¢) / price ) · opens sit 10 pts below
Max leverage shown for a fresh position · leverage = 1 / (1 − LTV)phoebe.markets

Lenders

Choose your risk. Losses have an order.

Two ERC-4626 vaults share one pool. When a position goes bad, the protocol works down a fixed ladder, and junior is paid in advance to stand in front of senior.

Senior protected

Paid first, impaired last. Losses reach senior only after the entire junior tranche is gone. Interest only, lower and steadier.

Junior first loss

Takes bad debt first. Paid for it: higher interest, plus 0.3% of every borrow and 2% of every liquidation. Can't withdraw below 20% of outstanding debt.

1 · Liquidation

Liquidator repays the debt and takes the shares. The bonus starts at 3% and rises 1% per hour, up to 15%, until someone takes it.

2 · Absorb

Too far gone to liquidate profitably? The pool takes the shares as inventory; the shortfall is written off against junior.

3 · Settlement

Anything left at resolution redeems on Polymarket's contract at the real payout. Debt is paid first.

Interest scales with utilization · new borrowing stops if junior coverage gets thinphoebe.markets

The hedge loop

1Split USDC.e into matched YES + NO pairs on Polymarket's contract: inventory with no market risk.
2A trader opens a position; the venue sells them shares at the oracle price ± 1.5%.
3Within a tick, the hedger buys the same amount back on Polymarket's order book: fill-or-kill, within 2¢.
4Polymarket down, region blocked or exposure unhedged? The venue switches itself off.
