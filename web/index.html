Φοίβη · “the radiant”

Phoebe was the Titaness of the moon, and the keeper of the oracle at Delphi before it passed to Apollo. Ours keeps an oracle too: every price lands on chain with its volatility, so leverage tightens before the market moves, not after.

The risk engine

How the risk engine sets leverage.

A position's liquidation LTV is the lowest of four limits, re-evaluated at every oracle update. The binding one is shown in the app, so you always know why your leverage is what it is.

Α

Price bands

No leverage within 3¢ of 0 or 1. It ramps to full between 15¢ and 85¢, where a share has room to move both ways.

0.03 · 0.15 — 0.85 · 0.97
Β

Time ramp

Scheduled resolutions are known jumps. Leverage fades to zero over the three days before a cutoff 24h ahead of them.

ramp 3d · cutoff T − 24h
Γ

Volatility + jump cap

The margin cushion must cover a two-sigma day plus a 10¢ gap, so calm markets that suddenly break stay covered.

LTV ≤ 1 − (2σ₁ᴅ + 0.10) / p
Δ

Absorb, never fire-sell

Underwater positions are written off against junior and their shares sold after a 6h cool-off. A Dutch-auction bonus keeps liquidators in thin books.

bonus 3% → +1%/h → 15%

Senior and junior tranches.

Choose your exposure. Losses run through junior before they can reach senior, and junior is paid for standing in front.

Senior

Paid interest first and impaired last. In the 60-market holdout backtest, senior was never impaired.

  • Borrow interest, first claim
  • Behind the whole junior tranche

losses land on junior first

junior · 20%first loss

Junior

Takes the first loss on bad debt, and collects the fees that pay for that risk: every opening fee and a cut of every liquidation.

  • 0.3% of every amount borrowed
  • 2% of every liquidation
